Standard Rental Agreement Template
RENTAL AGREEMENT
This agreement is made on this ______ day of _________, 20, between:
• First Party (Owner/Landlord): Name: ________________________, S/o, D/o, or W/o: ________________________, residing at __________________________________________________. Hereinafter called the Lessor/Owner.
• Second Party (Tenant): Name: ________________________, S/o, D/o, or W/o: ________________________, residing at __________________________________________________. Hereinafter called the Lessee/Tenant.
The expressions "Lessor/Owner" and "Lessee/Tenant" shall mean and include their legal heirs, successors, and assigns.
Key Terms and Conditions
1. Demised Premises: The Owner hereby lets out the residential property located at __________________________________________________ to the Tenant for residential use only.
2. Term of Tenancy: The lease is for a fixed period of ______ months/years, starting from _______________ and ending on _______________. It can be renewed upon mutual consent.
3. Monthly Rent: The Tenant agrees to pay a monthly rent of Rs. _________ (in words: ___________________________________) payable on or before the ______ day of each English calendar month.
4. Security Deposit: The Tenant has paid an interest-free refundable security deposit of Rs. _________ to the Owner. This amount will be refunded upon vacating the premises, after deducting any unpaid utility bills or damage costs.
5. Utilities and Maintenance: The Tenant is responsible for paying electricity and water consumption charges. Minor day-to-day maintenance shall be handled by the Tenant.
6. Termination: Either party may terminate this agreement by giving ______ month's prior written notice to the other party.
